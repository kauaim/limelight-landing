Services

Power consulting

We review your utility service, electrical distribution, and load profile to establish what the site can support today. We identify the constraints and lay out the options for added capacity or redundancy. You receive written recommendations that your engineers and contractors can work from.

Battery storage and backup

We assess where batteries fit on your site, whether for backup power, peak demand reduction, or support alongside generators and UPS systems. Systems are sized against your actual loads and required runtime. We also review vendor proposals and specifications before you commit.

Data center energy planning

We build a power plan from your load forecast, covering the first phase through full build-out. The plan addresses utility coordination, on-site generation and storage, and redundancy targets. Each phase ties capacity to your construction schedule and budget.

Cybersecurity services

We secure power and control systems, including OT/ICS networks and battery management systems. Our security assessments review network design, remote access, and device configuration. You receive a prioritized list of fixes for your operations team.

AI energy automation

We build load forecasts from your meter and equipment data. Automated battery dispatch uses those forecasts to set charge and discharge times within limits you define. Monitoring tracks load, battery state, and dispatch results, and alerts your operators to faults.
